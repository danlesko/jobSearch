# Job boards beyond LinkedIn and Indeed

**Research date:** September 23, 2026  
**Scope:** 19 high-signal job boards and nontraditional hiring channels, excluding LinkedIn and Indeed.

This list favors sites with a real niche, identifiable employers, meaningful filters or curation, and a credible path to the employer or its applicant-tracking system. It intentionally includes a few nontraditional channels—such as a hiring community and freelance marketplaces—because they expose opportunities that ordinary job-board searches often miss.

## Best starting set

For a broad professional search, start with **Wellfound**, **Built In**, **We Work Remotely**, and the relevant specialist board below. Add **USAJOBS** or **GovernmentJobs.com** for public-sector work, and **Climatebase**, **Idealist**, or **Work for Good** for mission-driven work.

## Curated list

| # | Board / channel | Best for | Why it is worth checking | Watch-outs |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com/jobs) | Startup, product, engineering, design, and operations roles | Startup-native profiles often show funding stage, salary, equity, remote policy, and direct founder or hiring-manager access. The live site reports startup roles and recently posted jobs. Formerly AngelList Talent. | Startup risk is real; some listings become stale. Confirm that the company is still actively hiring and verify the role on its careers page. |
| 2 | [Y Combinator Jobs / Work at a Startup](https://www.ycombinator.com/jobs) | YC-backed startups across technical and business functions | A concentrated pool of companies filtered through the YC ecosystem. Useful for early-stage roles that may never reach large generalist boards. | Limited to YC companies; compensation, runway, and management quality vary widely. |
| 3 | [Built In](https://builtin.com/jobs) | Technology companies, startups, and tech-adjacent roles | Strong technology taxonomy, employer profiles, tech-hub coverage, remote filters, salary information, and visibly fresh listings. | Coverage is strongest in the U.S.; many roles are hybrid or location-bound even when remote filters are available. |
| 4 | [Dice](https://www.dice.com/) | Software, IT, cybersecurity, data, engineering, and contract work | Long-running technology-specialist board with granular skills and role filters and significant enterprise and staffing-company participation. | Recruiter and staffing-agency listings are common. Check who the actual employer is before applying. |
| 5 | [We Work Remotely](https://weworkremotely.com/) | Fully remote roles, especially engineering, product, design, marketing, and support | A dedicated remote board with a long operating history, paid employer postings, role categories, and frequent new listings. | “Remote” can still mean a specific country, time zone, or contractor arrangement. Most applications leave the board for an external ATS. |
| 6 | [FlexJobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/) | Screened remote, hybrid, flexible, freelance, and part-time roles | Human-screened listings and strong scam/junk filtering make it useful for people who value signal over maximum volume. | Full access is paid; many roles also appear elsewhere. Treat screening as a useful filter, not a substitute for employer research. |
| 7 | [Remote OK](https://remoteok.com/) | Global remote technology and digital roles | Large remote-focused inventory with useful tags, salary signals on some posts, and strong visibility among remote-first employers. | More volume and aggregation than curation. Verify every promising job on the employer’s own site and watch for location restrictions. |
| 8 | [Climatebase](https://climatebase.org/jobs) | Climate tech, energy, sustainability, conservation, and impact roles | Sector-specific discovery across startups, nonprofits, fellowships, internships, contract work, and paid jobs, with recent-posting filters. | “Climate” is a broad label; investigate the employer’s actual impact, funding, and business model. |
| 9 | [Idealist](https://www.idealist.org/en/jobs) | Nonprofit, NGO, advocacy, education, foundation, and social-impact work | One of the strongest mission-driven networks, with jobs, internships, volunteer opportunities, and global nonprofit coverage. The site shows live recent postings. | Compensation and role scope vary significantly. Some results are unpaid or stipend-based, so read employment terms carefully. |
| 10 | [Work for Good](https://workforgood.org/) | Purpose-driven nonprofit professionals | Good filters for fundraising, conservation, advocacy, education, and social services, plus job alerts and featured roles. | Coverage may be incomplete while postings migrate between systems; use it alongside Idealist and target-organization career pages. |
| 11 | [USAJOBS](https://www.usajobs.gov/) | U.S. federal government careers | The authoritative federal civilian hiring portal, with structured eligibility, hiring-path, pay-grade, benefits, agency, and location information. | Applications are detailed and hiring timelines can be long. Federal resumes and eligibility rules differ from private-sector norms. |
| 12 | [GovernmentJobs.com](https://www.governmentjobs.com/jobs) | City, county, state, education, and other public agencies | Broad local-government coverage with agency, location, salary, and alert filters; many public employers use it as their recruiting system. | It is a third-party platform, not the government itself. Confirm deadlines and details with the hiring agency. |
| 13 | [Chronicle of Higher Education Jobs](https://jobs.chronicle.com/jobs/) | Faculty, research, higher-ed administration, and university leadership | Strong institutional signal and detailed academic filters for faculty, dean/provost, student affairs, research, enrollment, and staff roles. | Faculty hiring is seasonal and slow; some searches may have internal candidates. Check application deadlines closely. |
| 14 | [Health eCareers](https://lifescijobs.org/) | Physicians, nurses, advanced practitioners, allied health, and healthcare employers | Profession- and specialty-focused healthcare listings connected to medical-society and healthcare-employer networks; new jobs are added regularly. | Best for clinical/provider searches. Verify recruiter identity and the actual employing health system. |
| 15 | [PracticeLink](https://physicians.practicelink.com/) | Physicians, surgeons, nurse practitioners, and physician assistants | Deep specialty, facility, geography, and employer filters, with direct-recruiter contact and substantial active inventory. | Highly focused on physicians and advanced practice; not a general healthcare board. |
| 16 | [eFinancialCareers](https://www.efinancialcareers.com/) | Banking, investment management, quant, fintech, risk, compliance, and accounting | Global finance specialization, granular function filters, job alerts, and visible activity from major financial institutions and specialist recruiters. | Recruiter-sourced roles are common and some listings repeat. Confirm the end employer and role status. |
| 17 | [Arc](https://arc.dev/talent) | Vetted remote developers, designers, data professionals, and technical contractors | Matching-oriented access to remote full-time and contract work, with screening and a focus on experienced technical talent. | More of a talent marketplace than an open board; vetting favors experienced candidates and strong communication skills. |
| 18 | [Contra](https://contra.com/features/find-freelance-jobs) | Independent designers, engineers, marketers, writers, and consultants | Portfolio-first freelance opportunities, remote projects, direct client connection, contracts, invoicing, and commission-free payments; new opportunities are posted regularly. | Project work is not the same as stable employment. Some advanced opportunity access requires a paid plan, and competition can be high. |
| 19 | [Hacker News “Who Is Hiring?”](https://news.ycombinator.com/) | Technical candidates seeking founder-direct startup and software roles | Monthly discussion threads often contain unusual, direct-from-the-company roles in infrastructure, AI, developer tools, and early-stage startups. | It is an unstructured community thread, not a conventional board: little filtering, no standardized application workflow, and posts age quickly. Search Hacker News for the current “Who is Hiring?” thread. |

## How to use the list efficiently

1. Pick two channels that match the role and one channel that matches the work arrangement (for example, **eFinancialCareers + Built In + We Work Remotely**).
2. Save alerts using several title variants; niche boards often use different naming conventions than generalist sites.
3. Treat a board as discovery, then verify the posting on the employer’s official careers page before applying.
4. Prioritize fresh postings and direct-employer listings. Be especially cautious when a job asks for money, gift cards, cryptocurrency, banking credentials, or an interview entirely through an encrypted chat app.

- The shortlist was synthesized from four parallel Codex research lanes and two Claude research passes, then reconciled against live official-site results.
- Useful live checks included [Wellfound’s candidate overview](https://wellfound.com/candidates/overview), [Built In’s job search](https://builtin.com/jobs), [Climatebase](https://climatebase.org/), [Idealist jobs](https://www.idealist.org/en/jobs), [USAJOBS](https://www.usajobs.gov/?contentID=331), [Contra’s freelance-jobs page](https://contra.com/features/find-freelance-jobs), [BioSpace’s current hiring activity](https://www.biospace.com/now-hiring), and [eFinancialCareers’ current listings](https://www.efinancialcareers.com/).
- Avoid relying on low-signal aggregators as primary application channels. Sites such as Jooble, Talent.com, and SimplyHired can be useful for discovering an employer, but they commonly duplicate or repackage listings and should be followed back to the original employer.
